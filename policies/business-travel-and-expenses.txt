BUSINESS TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Synthetic training policy. Amounts and approval roles are fictional.

SCOPE

This policy covers employees and approved nonemployees who spend company funds for business travel, meals, lodging, transportation, supplies, or events. Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, accurately documented, and connected to a legitimate business purpose.

Travel to perform ordinary work from a personal vacation or an unapproved remote location is not business travel. Location approval remains governed by the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

APPROVAL

Overnight travel requires manager approval before booking. International travel also requires the department vice president, Information Security, and Travel Operations. Conference registration above $1,500 requires the cost-center owner.

Travelers submit the business purpose, destination, dates, expected customer or event, estimated cost, and any personal portion. Approval of a trip does not approve every expense. The traveler must follow category limits and use judgment.

BOOKING

Air, rail, hotel, and rental vehicles must be booked through the approved travel platform unless it cannot provide a safe or practical option. Economy airfare is required for flights under six scheduled hours. Premium economy may be approved for longer flights. Business class requires an accommodation or written executive approval.

Travelers should select a reasonably timed fare near the lowest logical cost, considering connections, ground transport, and productivity. Personal loyalty programs may be used but may not drive a materially higher company cost. Credits from company-paid travel belong to the company and should be used for later business travel.

LODGING AND TRANSPORTATION

Travelers should use preferred hotels within the city rate shown in the travel platform. A higher rate requires an explanation such as event hotel, accessibility, safety, or lower total transportation cost.

Public transportation and standard rideshare are preferred when safe and practical. Rental cars require a business need. Employees should decline optional rental insurance where company coverage applies and must not use a handheld phone while driving.

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the published location rate for business miles beyond the normal commute. Fuel is not reimbursed separately when mileage is claimed. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts when available.

MEALS

Reasonable meals are reimbursed during overnight travel and approved business meetings. The daily guideline is $75 in the United States, adjusted for high-cost cities in the travel platform. It is a guideline, not a per diem or entitlement. Alcohol is limited to two drinks per person when lawful, business appropriate, and permitted by the department.

The most senior employee present pays for a group meal to avoid self-approval. Attendee names and business purpose are required. Meals for ordinary local workdays are personal unless tied to approved overtime, travel, recruiting, customer activity, or a documented team event.

NONREIMBURSABLE ITEMS

Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, traffic or parking fines, commuting, childcare, pet care, clothing, upgrades for preference, and costs for personal companions are not reimbursable. Exceptions required as an accommodation are handled confidentially through HR.

Personal travel may be combined with business travel when it does not increase company cost or create tax, immigration, security, or insurance risk. The traveler pays incremental costs and clearly separates them in the itinerary and expense report.

EXPENSE REPORTS

Expense reports are due within 15 calendar days after the expense or trip ends. Itemized receipts are required for expenses of $25 or more and for lodging regardless of amount. A missing-receipt statement may be used only when the receipt cannot reasonably be obtained and repeated use may be reviewed.

Each line must identify date, merchant, amount, currency, category, attendees when relevant, and business purpose. Credit-card feeds do not replace receipts or explanation. Managers review within seven calendar days and must reject unsupported, duplicate, or personal items.

Foreign currency is converted using the corporate card amount or the approved expense-system rate for the transaction date. Travelers must not claim both a card charge and cash reimbursement for the same item.

CORPORATE CARDS AND ADVANCES

Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Accidental personal use must be reported and repaid promptly. Cardholders protect the card, review transactions, and report loss or suspected fraud immediately.

Cash advances are limited to destinations or activities where cards are not practical. Unused funds are returned and documented. New advances are not issued while a prior advance is overdue.

SAFETY AND DISRUPTION

Employees are not expected to travel into an unsafe situation. Travel Operations may restrict destinations or require security measures. In an emergency, the traveler should prioritize safety, contact local emergency services when appropriate, and notify the travel assistance line.

Reasonable costs caused by cancellation, severe delay, illness, or emergency are reimbursable with explanation. Employees should not work from another country during disruption unless international remote work is approved.

COMPLIANCE

False, altered, duplicate, or knowingly misleading claims may result in repayment and corrective action. Good-faith errors should be corrected promptly. Managers may not direct employees to misclassify expenses or split transactions to avoid approval limits.

Questions involving taxability, relocation, immigration, accommodations, gifts, government officials, or suspected fraud must be escalated to the appropriate specialist before reimbursement is promised.
