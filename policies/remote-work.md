# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> Synthetic training policy. It does not describe a real employer or create employment rights.

## Purpose and scope

RAGs to Riches Demo Company supports remote and hybrid work when it is compatible with the employee's role, location, performance, security duties, and team commitments. This policy applies to regular full-time and part-time employees. Contractors follow the location and security terms in their contracts. Remote work is an arrangement for where work is performed; it is not a reduction in scheduled hours, availability, productivity, or conduct expectations.

## Eligibility

An employee may request a hybrid arrangement after 90 calendar days of employment. A fully remote arrangement normally requires 180 calendar days of employment. The waiting period may be waived in writing by the Head of People for a business need, disability accommodation, or documented recruiting commitment.

The role must be classified as remote-capable in the job profile. Employees handling physical mail, laboratory equipment, on-site reception, or secure facilities may have role-specific limits. An employee must have a current performance rating of Meets Expectations or higher and no active final written warning. A performance improvement plan does not automatically end an existing arrangement, but the manager and HR must review whether additional on-site coaching is needed.

Eligibility does not guarantee approval. The manager considers customer coverage, collaboration, time-zone overlap, data access, and equitable staffing. HR validates employment, payroll, and location requirements before work begins in a new location.

## Approved locations

Employees may work only from the primary work location recorded in the HR system or another location approved in writing. A temporary domestic location of up to 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period may be approved by the manager and HR at least 10 business days in advance. Longer changes require a permanent location review.

International remote work is prohibited unless Legal, Information Security, Payroll, and HR give written approval before travel. A tourist visa, family residence, or prior informal practice is not approval. Employees must not begin work from an unapproved country while a request is pending.

The company currently supports regular remote employment only in locations listed as active in the synthetic location register. Moving to another state or country can change taxes, pay range, benefits, insurance, and labor-law obligations. Employees must submit a location change request at least 30 days before a planned move.

The synthetic location register contains California (US-CA), New York (US-NY), Texas (US-TX), Florida (US-FL), and London (GB-LND). Regular remote employment is supported only in California, New York, and Texas. Florida is in the register but is not supported for regular remote employment. London is international and is not supported for regular remote employment. A city, state, or country that is not one of those five register entries is outside the register. Naming that place is not the same as leaving the location blank; HR cannot approve regular remote work there from this list.

## Schedule and availability

Employees follow the work schedule recorded for their team. Unless the manager documents another window, hybrid and remote employees must be reachable from 10:00 a.m. through 3:00 p.m. in their assigned team time zone. Meal and rest breaks follow the employee's work location. Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked and receive approval before overtime.

Remote arrangements may include required on-site events. Employees assigned within 50 miles of an office may be asked to attend up to four planned on-site days per quarter with at least 10 business days' notice. Travel expectations for employees hired as fully remote are stated in the offer or role profile and are reimbursed under the Business Travel Policy.

## Workspace, equipment, and security

The workspace must be safe, private enough for the work performed, and supported by reliable internet. Company business must be conducted on managed devices when one is issued. Employees must use multifactor authentication, the approved password manager, screen locking, and the company VPN when required. Confidential discussions may not be held where unauthorized people can hear them. Paper containing personal or confidential information must be secured and destroyed through an approved method.

The company provides a standard laptop and approved accessories based on role. The employee is responsible for ordinary home internet and utilities unless local law or a written arrangement requires reimbursement. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on request or separation. Suspected loss, theft, phishing, or unauthorized access must be reported to the security channel immediately and no later than one hour after discovery.

## Request and approval process

The employee submits a request stating the requested schedule, primary work address, proposed start date, business coverage plan, and any equipment needs. The manager records a recommendation. HR checks tenure, performance status, role classification, and location support. Information Security reviews elevated-access roles. A request is approved only when the HR system contains a written decision and effective date.

Managers should respond within 10 business days after receiving a complete request. If more review is needed, the manager or HR provides a status update. A denial must identify a business or compliance reason and may propose a different schedule. Employees may request reconsideration by HR within 15 calendar days, supplying relevant new information.

## Review, changes, and exceptions

Arrangements are reviewed at least every six months and whenever the employee changes roles, managers, location, or performance status. The company may modify or end an arrangement for business coverage, performance, security, legal, or safety reasons. Except for urgent risk, the employee normally receives 15 calendar days' notice.

Medical and disability-related requests are handled under the Workplace Accommodations Policy, not decided solely under this policy. Employees should not include diagnoses in a normal remote-work request. HR will direct them to the confidential accommodation process.

Only the Head of People may approve an exception to tenure or review intervals. Legal and Payroll must approve exceptions involving an unsupported location. All exceptions must identify an owner, reason, start date, end date, and review date.

## Home office support

After a remote or hybrid arrangement is approved, the employee may request a one-time home-office setup amount of up to $500. The amount covers a desk, chair, monitor, or lamp bought for work. It does not cover rent, utilities other than the internet stipend, furniture for other household members, or renovations. The employee submits itemized receipts within 60 calendar days of the approval date. A second setup amount is not available unless the employee changes primary work location and HR approves a new arrangement.

The company also pays a $50 monthly internet stipend while the remote or hybrid arrangement is active. The stipend is added to pay and may be taxable. It stops at the end of the month in which the arrangement ends. Employees who need a monitor, keyboard, or headset that stays company property request it through Workplace Experience instead of the setup amount. Company property is returned when the arrangement or the employment ends.

## Meetings and availability

Employees keep core collaboration hours from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in the team's primary time zone, matching the workplace logistics policy. A camera is expected for team meetings unless the employee has an approved accommodation or the meeting host says cameras are optional. Backgrounds should not reveal confidential information. A coworker may use a blurred background.

If the home is too noisy or too crowded for a customer meeting, the employee may use a company coworking day pass on up to four days in a calendar month. The pass is booked in the Workplace Experience portal and must be in an approved work location. Coworking does not expand the location register. Children, pets, and other household members may be at home during the workday. They should not be asked to participate in meetings, and the employee remains responsible for customer confidentiality.
